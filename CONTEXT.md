# Card

A single-page personal business card for Ulysses Souza, Senior Software Engineer in Berlin. Its job is to make a hiring decision easier during a visit that lasts seconds.

## Language

**Card**:
The site itself: one page that presents Ulysses to a Visitor. The whole product.
_Avoid_: website, portfolio, homepage

**Visitor**:
The person the Card is built for: a recruiter or hiring manager deciding whether to contact Ulysses. Secondary audience: professional contacts met in person.
_Avoid_: user, reader, visitor of the site

**Proof**:
Content that demonstrates competence instead of claiming it: Selected Projects and Contributions.
_Avoid_: portfolio, work samples, experience

**Selected Project**:
A personal, non-fork repository featured on the Card as Proof.
_Avoid_: featured repo, showcase project

**Contribution**:
A merged pull request by Ulysses to an open-source project, cited on the Card as Proof.
_Avoid_: OSS work, commits, open-source activity

**Positioning**:
The one-line statement of who Ulysses is and what he works on, placed under the name.
_Avoid_: tagline, bio, summary

**Focus**:
Short tags naming the technical areas Ulysses works in.
_Avoid_: skills, tech stack, keywords

**Channel**:
A public, verified way to reach or check Ulysses: GitHub, LinkedIn, or email.
_Avoid_: link, social, contact method

**Employer Anonymity**:
The policy that the Card never states the employment relationship: no employer name, no CV block. It may name public projects and credentials ("ex-maintainer of Docker Compose"); the public record speaks for itself.
_Avoid_: privacy, "no company listed"
